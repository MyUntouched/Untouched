FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long is the contract?

Month to month. Cancel with a quick message, or pause for a slow month — no contract on anything.

What if the work isn't done?

If we don't deliver the month's work, you don't pay for that month.

Who does the work?

Us — Samuel and Janoy, two local people, not a call centre.

What do you need from me?

About 30 minutes a month to share photos, and a tap to approve posts.

What do you need from me to get started?

Add samuelyorke@myuntouched.com as a Manager on your Google page (you stay the owner — we never ask for your password), add us in Meta Business Suite for Instagram and Facebook, and send us your booking link, your customer email list and your unpaid invoices. For missed calls, you turn on "forward when no answer" on your shop phone and sign the phone registration in your shop's name. That's it — we take it from there.

How do I pay?

By Square invoice — we send the first one when you sign up, then one a month, on the same day each month.

What happens after the free chat?

We send you a short message within a day, with the website link and the 2–3 things we'd fix first. No pressure — you decide from there.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You add samuelyorke@myuntouched.com as a Manager on your Google page and in Meta Business Suite — you stay the owner, and we never ask for your password.

What happens in the first week?

We get set up — your Google page, your socials, your review replies — within the first week. Missed-call text-backs take a few days longer, while the phone registration clears.

What if I only want one thing fixed?

Take just that add-on — every service is available on its own.

What happens to my Google page if I cancel?

It stays yours. Everything we built on it stays, and you keep the login.
